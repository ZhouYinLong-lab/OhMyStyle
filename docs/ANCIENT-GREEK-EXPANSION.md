# Ancient Greek expansion

The Ancient Greek direction is represented as a chronological family rather
than a single prompt. The four packages share a historical lineage but expose
different reproduction constraints:

| Package | Approximate period | Reproduction anchor |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Geometric](../style-packages/movements/greek-geometric-period/) | c. 900–700 BCE | registers, angular signs, terracotta, repeated motifs |
| [Archaic](../style-packages/movements/greek-archaic-period/) | c. 700–480 BCE | contour narrative, ceramic fields, patterned naturalism |
| [Classical](../style-packages/movements/greek-classical-period/) | c. 480–323 BCE | proportion, balance, measured weight shift, lucid space |
| [Hellenistic](../style-packages/movements/greek-hellenistic-period/) | c. 323–31 BCE | torsion, diagonal force, pathos, varied bodies |

These dates and the visual transitions are research scaffolds based on the
Metropolitan Museum of Art's overviews of Geometric, Archaic, Classical, and
Hellenistic art. They are not claims that a period had one uniform style.

Historical works stay link-only by default. Generated samples are stored under
each package's `examples/generated/` directory with a sidecar recording the
anonymous prompt mode and human-review status.
