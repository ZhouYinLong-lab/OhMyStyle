## Overview

This package extracts a frontal, clear, typological documentary approach. Clothing, posture, tools, and surroundings contribute to the portrait alongside the face. The camera does not chase theatrical expression; it lets social identity and material detail emerge slowly.

## Curatorial note

There is an archival patience here: where a person stands, what they wear, and what they hold matter as much as their face. The representative image uses an anonymous worker to show the relationship, but the package does not require workers or uniforms. Transfer the way subject and context share information, not a black-and-white filter.

## Subject independence

The package controls documentary observation, posture, environmental proportion, light, and tone. It does not require workers, professions, uniforms, German locations, or frontal portraits. The person and tool are examples only; the user's subject remains authoritative.

## Source and rights

The package refers to [the August Sander Foundation biography](https://augustsander.org/page/biography) for research and extracts observable photographic characteristics only. External photographs remain with their rights holders. The representative image is an original anonymous scene, not a reproduction or endorsement. See [`provenance.yaml`](provenance.yaml), [`references/manifest.csv`](references/manifest.csv), and the root [`NOTICE`](../../../NOTICE).

## Use only this package

1. **Give it to an image-capable Agent:** provide this directory and ask the Agent to read the identity, visual signature, reproduction, prompts, palette, and evaluation files before compiling your subject-specific prompt.
2. **Copy the Prompt:** replace `{SUBJECT}` and `{LOCATION}` in `prompts/base.txt`, and submit `prompts/negative.txt` as the negative prompt.
3. **Use your own API key:** submit the base prompt, negative constraints, palette, and relevant references through your chosen service. This repository does not host keys or image generation.
4. **Use a local model + ComfyUI:** wire the prompts into your workflow, reproduce the posture, context, tone, and material-detail rules, then review with `evaluation.yaml`.

Models, keys, and generated images remain under the user's control. References are for feature analysis, not copying.
