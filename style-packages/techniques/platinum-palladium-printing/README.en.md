## Overview

This package simulates the material experience of platinum–palladium printing: the image seems absorbed into matte fiber paper, grayscale remains continuous, shadows are deep but open, highlights are soft, and the whole print may carry a restrained warm-neutral shift. It describes a process, not a required portrait or landscape subject.

## Curatorial note

The beauty does not come from an antique border or a brown filter. It comes from the absence of a hard layer between image and paper. The subject can be contemporary; light, paper, and grayscale simply have to work together. A quiet surface can still retain small traces of exposure, coating, and material absorption.

## Subject independence

This package controls print surface, grayscale, and process character, not people, architecture, objects, places, or historical period.

## Use only this package

1. **Image-capable Agent:** upload this directory. Ask the Agent to read the process rules, confirm your subject, generate, and review the result.
2. **Copy the prompts:** replace subject, location, aspect ratio, and purpose in `prompts/base.txt`, then submit the negative constraints.
3. **Use your own API:** configure your API key and submit the prompt, negative constraints, palette, and reference links.
4. **Local model and ComfyUI:** connect the prompts, use the reproduction notes for grayscale, paper, and light, then review the output.

References are for observable traits only. Do not copy a source work’s composition, figures, text, trademark, or logo.
