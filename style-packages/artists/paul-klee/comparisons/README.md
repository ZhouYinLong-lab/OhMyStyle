# Comparison assets

- `legacy.png`: a tabletop fan rendered with the existing Klee package and no art direction.
- `direction.png`: the same requested subject rendered with `klee-independent-line-atmospheric-color`.
- `legacy-job.json` and `direction-job.json`: provider-neutral compiler jobs.
- `generation-prompts.md`: prompts, visual review, and limitations.

The images are anonymous generated demonstrations, not reproductions of MoMA's *Twittering Machine*. Review whether the fan stays identifiable, whether the motion line extends beyond its contour, whether line and color operate independently, and whether the paper remains visible.

The new-direction image shows a distinct extended motion line and a concentrated blue-violet field. It still renders the fan too realistically and the field is not cleanly independent from the object; see `generation-prompts.md` for the full review. The legacy image was restyled from the direction image to keep the subject and composition close.
