# Prompts and visual review

The provider-neutral compiler jobs are included as `legacy-job.json` and `direction-job.json`. The prompts below describe the anonymous images shown here.

## New direction image

> Create an original horizontal 16:9 watercolor, gouache, and ink drawing on pale textured paper. Subject exactly: one small brass-cage desk fan with a dark wooden pedestal, standing on a plain side table and turned slightly to the side; its blades are gently turning. Keep the fan recognizable as one object and preserve the brass cage and dark pedestal. Draw its structure with a few thin, wiry black ink lines; let one graceful line indicating the fan's motion travel beyond its silhouette into open paper. Add one irregular translucent blue-violet watercolor field behind and partly crossing the fan, independent from the ink contour, with a small muted ochre accent. Leave large areas of paper empty. Include only a few faint incidental ink-transfer smudges. No people, birds, musical notes, letters, signs, extra objects, enclosing frame, photorealism, CAD blueprint, text, signature, or watermark.

## Legacy package comparison

> Restyle the direction image for a controlled comparison with the broad, legacy Paul Klee package. Preserve the exact same brass-cage desk fan, dark wooden pedestal, plain side table, orientation, motion state, composition and horizontal 16:9 framing. Change only the treatment: make the long line less like a single smooth airflow trail and instead let a few lighter, wandering hand-drawn lines connect and pass between the fan and several translucent, softly overlapping ochre, rose, turquoise and violet color blocks. Allow line and colored shapes to mingle more generally across the composition, with a playful symbolic rhythm and shallow space; keep the fan recognizable but let it participate in the overall pattern. Maintain a handmade watercolor, gouache and ink on paper look with visible pale paper. Do not add birds, notes, letters, writing, extra objects, or any recognizable copied arrangement from a named artwork. No text, signature or watermark.

## Review

- **Held close:** both images show the same brass-cage fan, wooden pedestal, side table, orientation and wide composition. The legacy image was restyled from the new-direction image; this is a controlled visual comparison, not two independent generations.
- **Visible distinction:** the direction probe concentrates a single long action line beyond the fan and a blue-violet field behind it. The legacy probe distributes thin lines and translucent geometric color blocks across more of the frame.
- **Missed constraint:** the new-direction probe depicts the fan with more realistic metallic detail than requested and uses a large wash that is not especially independent of its object. The legacy edit keeps much of that realism too.
- **Status:** the line path and field placement are visibly different, but the outputs do not yet demonstrate consistent stylization control. All rule-level visual review remains pending; keep this direction experimental and test on other mechanical subjects.

These are anonymous generated demonstrations, not reproductions of MoMA's *Twittering Machine*. The legacy image is a restyling of the direction image, not an independent baseline.
