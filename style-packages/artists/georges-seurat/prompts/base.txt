Create a new oil painting of an unfamiliar riverside public garden on a bright afternoon. Use stable horizontal bands, measured spacing between quietly posed figures, repeated trees or shoreline markers, and calm readable silhouettes. Build the entire surface with disciplined small adjacent color units: blue, green, yellow, vermilion, violet, and deep blue, allowing optical mixing at viewing distance. Shadows remain chromatic and structured. The people, park, and arrangement must be new; do not imitate a famous park painting.
