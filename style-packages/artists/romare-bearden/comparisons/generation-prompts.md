# Generation prompts and visual review

## Shared subject

A fictional urban street with exactly three adjoining storefronts: a bakery, a bicycle repair shop, and a small bookshop. Exactly two fully clothed adult pedestrians walk along one continuous sidewalk. No readable signs or named place, horizontal 16:9.

## Base package image

Use the package's cut and torn colored papers, painted planes, visible seams, overlapping fragments, deep blue, brick red, mustard yellow, cream, and black. Keep the three storefronts in one continuous broad street composition without imposing repeated vignettes.

## Direction image

Edit the base image. Preserve exactly the same three storefronts and their order, the same two pedestrians, colors, frame, and sidewalk. Separate the three fronts into adjacent but slightly offset paper-cut vignettes with visible seams and irregular top edges. Keep a single uninterrupted sidewalk band below them. Retain only shallow, bounded views through the storefront windows.

## Review

- **Subject preservation:** both images retain a bakery, bicycle repair shop, bookshop, and two distinct pedestrians in the same left-to-right order.
- **Visible rule:** the base image reads as one continuous facade. The direction image has three differently raised paper-cut scene modules; the uninterrupted sidewalk remains the shared anchor.
- **Boundary check:** store interiors remain within windows. No extra named location or Harlem-specific institution is introduced.
- **Method limitation:** the direction sample edits the base image so subject and palette stay close. Test other segment counts and different user-provided neighborhoods before promoting the direction; formal review remains pending.

## Source boundary

The [Met collection record for *The Block*](https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/481891) and its [exhibition notes](https://www.metmuseum.org/exhibitions/listings/2010/romare-bearden) describe an urban collage organized as connected street vignettes and six panels. This direction extracts only the general idea of a segmented street sequence; it does not reproduce the six-panel structure, Harlem, named institutions, or the source composition. The source image is not used as a generation input.
