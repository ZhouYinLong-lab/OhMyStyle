# Prompts sent to the image generator

These are the generation prompts used for the comparison samples. The matching compiled jobs are in `legacy-job.json` and `direction-job.json`.

## Legacy package

> Landscape 16:9 oil painting. Show exactly one white ceramic cup on a plain tabletop, and nothing else as a distinct object. Preserve the simple cup silhouette and plain tabletop; add no people, symbols, lettering, ornamented setting, or narrative. Follow this broad legacy package language: fine directional lines and decorative curves; dense jewel-blue, deep-red, gold, and dark-green color layers; flat detail layered with dreamlike space; precise local detail within a mysterious overall surface rhythm. Set the cup clearly in front of the tabletop, with a new composition and no copied artwork, watermark, or logo.

## New art direction

> Landscape 16:9 oil painting. Show exactly one white ceramic cup on a plain tabletop, with no other distinct objects. Preserve the cup's clear silhouette and plain tabletop; no people, symbols, text, or narrative elements. Apply a Moreau-inspired direction through a few precise sinuous contour lines and restrained arabesque accents that follow the cup's outline, jewel-like blue, deep red, and dark green planes with strong value separation, and deliberate small shifts between drawn contour and color edges. Keep most of the tabletop quiet; concentrate fine ornamental detail around a limited area of the cup and leave surrounding space subtly mysterious but still a plain tabletop. No generic swirls, no enclosing border, no all-over gold or filigree, no fantasy temple or landscape, no copied artwork, watermark, or logo.
