Create an original expressionist oil painting of {SUBJECT} in {LOCATION}. Treat
the setting as a psychological landscape: compress or gently tilt the space, let
shorelines, paths, rails, branches, or sky bands become long undulating rhythms,
and allow the environment to share the figure's tension. Use rust red, acid
ochre, deep blue, green-black, and unstable dirty flesh tones; non-natural color
must express an inner condition rather than decorate the scene. Simplify detail
into symbolic masses and retain broad uneven loaded marks, enclosing contours,
and visible support. The figure is anonymous and psychologically specific. Make
a new scene and pose, not an iconic scream, famous composition, or horror filter.
