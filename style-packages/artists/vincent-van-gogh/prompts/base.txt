Create an original oil painting of {SUBJECT} in {LOCATION}. Build the image
from clearly observed color masses, then cover them with thick, visible,
directional brush marks. Use saturated blue-violet and ultramarine passages
against warm yellow and ochre accents, with olive greens and chromatic umber
anchors. Let the marks describe motion: short curved strokes in the sky or
foliage, slanted strokes across the ground, and contour-following strokes around
organic forms. Keep the subject readable, the space layered, and the focal area
more mark-dense than the quiet edges. Highlights must be loaded paint, not glow;
shadows must remain colored rather than black. Preserve irregular human brush
decisions and tactile oil thickness. This is a new scene with a new arrangement,
not a reconstruction of any known artwork.
