## Overview

This package extracts repeated bands, curves, changing intervals, and black-white or limited-color relationships that activate visual perception. It focuses on the active relationship between a flat plane and the viewer, not on a particular object or literal 3D illusion.

## Curatorial note

The image does not need a story to hold attention: spacing becomes the movement. A band tightens and the eye seems to accelerate; a curve turns and the plane appears to shift. The representative image keeps that strict but not entirely mechanical rhythm. When the subject changes, transfer repetition, interval, and contrast—not an automatic stripe, checkerboard, or tunnel.

## Subject independence

The package controls flat composition, repetition, color relationships, and surface. It does not require a person, object, place, or story. The abstract bands are an example only; user instructions remain authoritative.

## Source and rights

The package refers to [MoMA's material on Bridget Riley's Current](https://www.moma.org/audio/playlist/297/4821) for research and extracts observable visual characteristics only. The representative image is an original abstract image, not a reproduction or endorsement. See [`provenance.yaml`](provenance.yaml), [`references/manifest.csv`](references/manifest.csv), and the root [`NOTICE`](../../../NOTICE).

## Use only this package

1. **Give it to an image-capable Agent:** provide this directory and ask the Agent to read the identity, visual signature, reproduction, prompts, palette, and evaluation files before compiling your subject-specific prompt.
2. **Copy the Prompt:** replace `{SUBJECT}` and `{LOCATION}` in `prompts/base.txt`, and submit `prompts/negative.txt` as the negative prompt.
3. **Use your own API key:** submit the base prompt, negative constraints, palette, and relevant references through your chosen service. This repository does not host keys or image generation.
4. **Use a local model + ComfyUI:** wire the prompts into your workflow, reproduce the repetition, interval, flatness, and contrast rules, then review with `evaluation.yaml`.

Models, keys, and generated images remain under the user's control. References are for feature analysis, not copying.
