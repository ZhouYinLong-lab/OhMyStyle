- `direction.png`: the same subject rendered with `kusama-object-bounded-dot-field`.
- `legacy-job.json` and `direction-job.json`: provider-neutral compiler jobs.

These are anonymous generated demonstrations, not copies of Kusama's paintings or installations. Check that dots stay on the requested teapot, follow its rounded form, and preserve its handle, opening, spout, and silhouette.

In this probe the legacy dot field spreads onto the table and wall; the new direction confines it to the teapot and leaves the surrounding planes clear. The pot remains recognizable, though its glossy finish is not the intended hand-painted surface. See `generation-prompts.md` for review limits.
