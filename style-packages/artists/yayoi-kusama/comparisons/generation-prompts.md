# Prompts and visual review

The provider-neutral compiler jobs are included in this folder. The anonymous images used these prompts.

## Legacy package

> Original horizontal 16:9 still life with exactly one rounded mustard-yellow ceramic teapot: short spout, loop handle, and visible lid. It stands alone on a pale blue-gray table against an off-white wall. Cover the teapot, table, and wall with an expanding repetition of small irregular white dots, so the pattern gradually occupies the surrounding room surfaces while the teapot remains recognizable. Use a contemporary repeated-mark treatment with slight hand-applied variation, clean color blocks, matte painted surfaces, and visual accumulation. Keep it a simple still life, not an installation room. No other objects, pumpkins, soft sculptures, mirrors, text, logos, watermark, or copy of any named artwork.

## New direction image

> Edit the image to localize the white dot pattern while preserving the exact same yellow ceramic teapot, spout, loop handle, lid, pale blue-gray table, off-white wall, lighting, camera, scale, and horizontal composition. Remove every white dot from the wall and table so those surfaces become quiet and plain. Keep the white dots only on the teapot's ceramic body, lid, handle, spout, and knob; make them follow the rounded surfaces, slightly tighten spacing and narrow dot shapes as the form turns away, and stop cleanly at the teapot silhouette. Preserve the openings and negative space inside the handle, the lid seam, spout opening, and clear vessel contour. Keep slight hand-painted variation in dot shapes and spacing. No extra objects, pumpkins, mirrors, installation features, text, signature, or watermark.

## Review

- **Held close:** both images retain one yellow teapot with the same spout, loop handle, lid, pale table, wall, scale, and view. The direction image is an image-to-image edit of the legacy sample, not an independent generation.
- **Visible distinction:** the legacy sample spreads white dots across the wall and table; the new direction confines the dots to the teapot and leaves clear negative space around its silhouette.
- **Rule check:** the teapot, lid seam, spout, and handle remain identifiable; the dots stay within its outer contour and vary slightly with the rounded surface.
- **Remaining issue:** the object finish is glossy and more like a polished render than a matte hand-painted surface. This image probe tests pattern placement, not fidelity to Kusama's full medium range or repeatability.
- **Status:** the bounded-pattern direction is visibly demonstrated on this subject, but keep formal rule review pending until tested on other object shapes and materials.

These are anonymous demonstrations, not copies of Kusama's artwork or installation environments.
