# Generation prompts and visual review

## Shared subject

A nonrepresentational abstract painting with a quiet open center, an incomplete pale-blue arc, scattered red, ochre, black, and ivory geometric marks in asymmetric rhythm; tactile oil-painted surface; no recognizable objects; horizontal 16:9.

## Base package image

Use the package's broad abstract treatment: a pale mineral ground, circles, lines, arcs, geometric planes, asymmetrical color weight, varied hand-painted marks, tactile layered pigment, and a balance of dense and open areas. This sample does not load the optional art direction.

## Direction image

Edit the base image while preserving the same tactile oil surface, ivory ground, cobalt, red, ochre, black, and muted green palette, horizontal framing, and nonrepresentational forms. Reorganize the composition around one large incomplete orbital arc enclosing a clearly quiet open center. Keep the ring broken, not concentric or target-like. Move a small uneven cluster of thin black lines and a few colored geometric marks to one side, with a smaller counterweight on the opposite edge and broad breathing space inside. Preserve asymmetrical painted tension, varied hand-made edges, and canvas texture. Add no planets, eyes, clocks, literal orbit diagram, labels, or text.

## Review

- **Subject preservation:** both images stay nonrepresentational and preserve the painted ground, broad palette, and abstract mark vocabulary.
- **Visible rule:** the direction edit gives the open center a dominant broken ring and moves angular marks into an asymmetrical perimeter cluster, leaving a wide quiet interior.
- **Remaining limitation:** the edit changes the overall placement and size of forms, so it demonstrates a compositional direction rather than a controlled local change. The ring still reads nearly closed in places; its opening could be made more explicit in another test. Formal visual review stays pending.
- **Method:** image-to-image edit keeps medium and palette close, but does not establish repeatability across models, aspect ratios, or independent generations.
