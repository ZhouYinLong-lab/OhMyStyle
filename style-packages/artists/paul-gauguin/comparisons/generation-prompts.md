# Prompts and visual review

The compiler jobs in this folder provide the reproducible, provider-neutral prompts. These exact image-generation prompts were used to produce the anonymous visual probes.

## New direction image

> Generate an original 16:9 oil painting demonstration, same exact scene as a companion comparison: one adult gardener wearing plain blue work clothes, standing beside a small flowering plant and holding pruning shears in a quiet garden. Make a distinctive synthetist/post-impressionist treatment: define the gardener's entire silhouette with a continuous strong dark violet-blue contour; make clothing and body mostly broad flat color areas with very few internal strokes; use just one clearly bounded patch of mottled broken brushwork on the garden ground behind the plant, with the rest of the setting in simplified large color shapes. Clear readable figure separated from background, neutral garden, no extra people. Do not add Breton, Polynesian, religious, mythological or exoticizing motifs. New composition, not a reproduction. No text, signature or watermark.

## Legacy package comparison

> Edit the supplied direction image into the legacy broad Gauguin package treatment for a controlled before/after comparison. Preserve the exact same adult gardener, identity presentation, blue work clothes, pruning shears, small flowering plant, pose, framing, garden objects and 16:9 composition. Change only the visual treatment: remove the heavy continuous dark outline around the figure; use the broader legacy approach of overlapping flattened color areas, more uniformly visible brushwork across figure and garden, compressed decorative color rhythm and integrated figure-ground handling. Keep the same palette family without the sharply separated flat figure. Do not add any motifs, symbols, additional figures, text, signature or watermark.

## Review

- **Held close:** both images retain one blue-clothed gardener, pruning shears, a small flowering plant, and the same broad garden arrangement. The legacy version was derived from the direction image, so this is a visual comparison rather than two independent image-generation trials.
- **Visible distinction:** the direction image uses a pronounced dark contour around the figure and more continuous blue fields in the clothes. The legacy image has less contour separation and more consistently broken brushwork.
- **Missed constraint:** the direction image still has mottled marks across much of the garden, not just one bounded patch. Its clothing also contains more modeling and brush strokes than the written rule asks for.
- **Status:** visual review remains pending for the direction itself. This single probe does not establish repeatable behavior; keep the rule experimental and evaluate more subjects before promoting it.

The generated samples are anonymous demonstrations, not copies of the cited museum work. The legacy image is an image-to-image restyling of the direction sample, not an independently randomized baseline.
