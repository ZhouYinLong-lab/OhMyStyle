# Comparison assets

- `legacy.png`: the gardener-and-flower scene compiled with the existing Gauguin package and no art direction.
- `direction.png`: the same subject compiled with `gauguin-outlined-figure-flat-ground`.
- `legacy-job.json` and `direction-job.json`: provider-neutral compiler jobs.
- `generation-prompts.md`: prompts, review, and limitations.
- The two PNGs are anonymous generated illustrations; the legacy image was restyled from the direction image to keep its scene, figure, and gesture close for comparison.

These are anonymous generated demonstrations, not copies of Met collection works. The direction image has a clearer dark figure contour and broad blue clothing fields, but the model still distributes mottled brushwork across much of the garden rather than confining it to one patch. This comparison is an initial visual probe, not validation that the rule reliably controls texture placement.
