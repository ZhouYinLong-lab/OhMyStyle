# Prompts and visual review

Compiled jobs are saved as `legacy-job.json` and `direction-job.json`. The paired images use the following generation and edit prompts.

## Legacy package

> Original horizontal 16:9 oil painting of exactly two adult dancers rehearsing a turning lift on a bare stage. One wears plain deep blue rehearsal clothes; the other wears an ochre wrap skirt. One warm stage lamp is mounted high at the upper right and is the only light source; the surrounding rehearsal studio stays dim. Preserve natural anatomy, contact, and the action of a supported turn. Use a Baroque painting treatment in general terms: diagonal energy in the two bodies, richly modeled volumes, warm highlights, deep colored shadows, supple brush edges, and thick and thin oil paint. Let both dancers be moderately illuminated by the same warm source, with gradated light across the scene. No audience, theater architecture, mythological or religious imagery, winged figures, added props, text, logo, watermark, or copying a named painting.

## New direction image

> Edit the lighting only for a controlled art-direction comparison. Preserve exactly the same two adult dancers, their faces and identities, blue rehearsal clothes, ochre wrap skirt, turning-lift pose, contact points, stage lamp in upper right, dark studio, and horizontal framing. Keep the single lamp as the only light source. Change the broad even stage illumination into one clearly visible diagonal sweep of warm amber light from upper right across the dancers toward lower left. Within the two figures, illuminate only selected planes facing the lamp: the dancer's face and upper shoulder, the partner's upturned face and forearm, and a few ridges of the ochre skirt. Let other limbs, the blue clothing, the overlap between bodies, and much of the stage remain in deep colored shadow. The highlighted patches should form a broken diagonal sequence that reinforces the existing turn while leaving each pose and contact readable. Keep warm painterly oil treatment, but avoid full-figure spotlight, glowing outlines, extra beams, flattening all shadows to black, changing anatomy, or adding objects. No text, signature or watermark.

## Review

- **Held close:** both images show the same two adult dancers, clothes, turning-lift contact, single upper-right lamp, dark stage, and wide framing. The direction image was edited from the legacy image, so this is one controlled probe rather than an independent repeat.
- **Visible distinction:** the new direction shows a stronger diagonal beam and more selective light on faces, the ochre skirt, and a small floor area; the blue clothing and spaces between the figures remain darker. The legacy sample spreads warm illumination more broadly across the dancers.
- **Missed constraint:** both samples are more photographic and smoothly rendered than the oil-painting package intends. The light edit also preserves broad illumination on much of the ochre garment.
- **Status:** the light-path difference is visible in this probe, but the painterly-medium adherence and repeatability remain unverified. Keep the direction experimental and review on other subjects.

These anonymous images are not copies of *The Fall of Phaeton* or any other Rubens work.
