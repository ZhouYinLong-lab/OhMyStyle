Create an original oil painting of {SUBJECT} in {LOCATION}. Treat weather and
light as active subjects. Build a spacious atmospheric field of blue-gray,
pearl-gray, pale cream, and subdued umber, then introduce one amber or muted
orange light event. Use a clear diagonal spatial vector through water, a wake,
smoke, a road, or a beam of weather. Forms should emerge and dissolve through
thin translucent veils and scumbled paint: distant structures lose contrast,
while only the narrative focal event receives sharper broken marks. Suggest rain,
mist, vapor, or sea air through layered paint rather than digital blur or lens
bloom. Keep the image physically believable, luminous but not glossy, and make a
new arrangement that does not reconstruct any known artwork.
