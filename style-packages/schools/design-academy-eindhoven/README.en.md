## Overview

This package extracts material experimentation, critical questions, social context, and prototype logic. It guides an image toward discussable design research rather than a generic product advertisement or polished future concept.

## Curatorial note

Good design research does not rush to hide its tests. Roughness, connection, and consequence should remain visible. The subject can be an everyday object, a space, or an abstract question; the important shift is that the image feels like it is asking something, not presenting a standard answer.

## Subject independence

This package controls visual treatment, not product type, exhibition hall, furniture, or sustainability symbol. The example prototype only demonstrates material research.

## Sources and rights

Observable traits were studied from the [official vision and mission](https://www.designacademy.nl/page/6083/vision-mission) and [official Graduation Show page](https://www.designacademy.nl/page/5937/graduation-show). Student projects and school imagery remain with their rights holders; this repository does not copy a specific work, logo, or exhibition layout.

## Use only this package

1. **Image-capable Agent:** upload this directory. Ask the Agent to read the rules, confirm your design subject, generate, and review the result.
2. **Copy the prompts:** replace subject, location, aspect ratio, and purpose in `prompts/base.txt`, then submit the negative constraints.
3. **Use your own API:** configure your API key and submit the prompt, negative constraints, palette, and reference links.
4. **Local model and ComfyUI:** connect the prompts, use the reproduction notes for material, structure, light, and detail, then review the output.

References are for observable traits only. Do not copy a student project’s composition, objects, text, trademark, or logo.
