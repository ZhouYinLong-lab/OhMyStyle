A three-quarter view of an unnamed moment of struggle, rescue, grief, or
public spectacle involving two or three bodies of different ages or physical
conditions. Drive the composition through one strong diagonal and a visible
counterforce; use torsion, reaching, turning, contact, foreshortening, and
drapery that amplifies motion. Make the scene empathetic and materially
credible as a marble or bronze group with selective polish, worn edges, dark
recesses, and restrained mineral red, blue, green, and ochre traces. Let the
space open toward the viewer without becoming a movie poster. No named
sculpture, no named artist, no gore, no fantasy armor, no logo or text.
