A monumental wall fresco showing an unnamed gathering of teachers, students,
and thinkers discussing ideas inside a grand symmetrical classical interior.
Build a strong one-point perspective with a central vanishing point beneath a
coffered barrel vault, repeated arches, tall columns, a broad staircase, and a
distant daylight opening. Place two calm central figures on the axis and arrange
balanced left and right groups around them; each group performs a distinct
intellectual action such as teaching geometry, reading, debating, measuring, or
writing. Use proportioned idealized anatomy, contrapposto, measured gestures,
clear faces, and broad gravity-following drapery. Paint as luminous buon fresco
with aged plaster, muted ultramarine, mineral red, ochre, olive, and deep
architectural shadow. The whole wall must read as one rational, serene,
humanist composition. No named historical figures, no copied fresco, no modern
text, no logo, no watermark, no baroque chaos, no fantasy temple, no cinematic
lens effects.
