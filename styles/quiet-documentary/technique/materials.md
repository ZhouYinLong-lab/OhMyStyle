# Materials and capture cues

For photography, “materials” describes the surfaces and light behavior that
should remain believable:

- natural wood, paper, cotton, wool, worn paint, glass, and matte ceramics;
- window light, overcast daylight, or a single practical lamp;
- skin texture and fabric weave retained at a natural scale;
- modest lens character rather than simulated plastic sharpness.

Do not add film grain as decoration after making the image excessively clean.
Grain should support the exposure and surface story.
