# Reproduction process

1. Choose a real or plausible everyday situation with one clear subject.
2. Establish the available light before adding any stylized effect.
3. Select a 35–50 mm equivalent viewpoint and keep the camera near eye level.
4. Compose for one readable subject and one meaningful environmental layer.
5. Lower saturation and contrast gently; keep skin and material colors credible.
6. Add only subtle grain and sharpening after tonal balance is stable.
7. Run the accepted/rejected checks before publishing a new example.
