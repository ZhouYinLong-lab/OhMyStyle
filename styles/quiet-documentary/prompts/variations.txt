Variation A — interior portrait:
Place the subject near a side-lit window in a lived-in room. Keep the room
visible enough to explain the subject's daily context.

Variation B — still life:
Arrange a small group of used objects on a table with one empty region. Let
material wear and soft directional light carry the visual interest.

Variation C — street observation:
Photograph an ordinary moment from a respectful distance. Keep signage and
identifying marks generic or remove them entirely.
