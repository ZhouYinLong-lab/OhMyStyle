# Composition rules

## Primary structure

- Keep the camera at eye level or slightly above the subject.
- Prefer medium shots, environmental portraits, and quiet still lifes.
- Leave one intentional area of negative space; it may be a wall, floor, sky,
  or an unoccupied part of a table.
- Let the subject sit slightly off-center unless the scene itself demands
  symmetry.
- Preserve foreground, middle ground, and background relationships without
  forcing theatrical depth.
- Allow ordinary objects to remain in the frame when they explain the place.

## Camera distance

Use enough distance for the environment to carry meaning. Avoid the compressed
look of a long telephoto lens and the exaggerated near-field distortion of an
ultra-wide lens.

## Negative-space test

The image should still communicate its mood if the subject is covered with a
simple mask. If every region is equally busy, the composition has drifted away
from this package.
